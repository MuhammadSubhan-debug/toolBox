QR Code Generator

Create multiple QR codes with custom designs, optional logos, and automatic social profile badges. Download individual PNGs or the whole batch as a ZIP.

One line creates one QR code. Paste multiple links or text lines to generate a batch.

Your QR code previews will appear here.

How to use

Enter one item per line to create multiple QR codes. Choose a design, upload your own logo, or enable automatic social platform badges. Download a single PNG or all generated codes in a ZIP. Scan every QR code before sharing.

QR generation runs in your browser. Your entered content is not uploaded. The QR and ZIP libraries load from a CDN, so an internet connection is required the first time.
